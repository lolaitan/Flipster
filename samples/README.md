# Sample pages

`scans/page_01.jpg` … `page_20.jpg` are the 20 hand-drawn notebook pages from the
original 15-112 term project, scanned with Adobe Scan and downscaled to 1400 px tall.
The stick figure walks to a box, pushes it off the page and walks off after it.

They are deliberately messy test data: every page has a different crop, there are
blue ruled lines, a red margin, binder holes that switch sides after page 10, and
blue ink bleeding through from the back of the paper.
